# Who reopens the Ridge Line

## Question

Who decides when the Ridge Line reopens in spring, and on what check?

## Angle

The reopening is one signature after one inspection, not a thaw.

## Promise

You will know who signs the reopening and what they check first.

## What this piece will not say

- That the 2026 date can be predicted.
- That snow never delays the sign-off.

## Sources

- Council minutes, 12 March 2019, https://example.org/ridge-line/council-minutes-2019: the drainage engineer signs the reopening after an inspection.
- Ridge Line maintenance plan, https://example.org/ridge-line/maintenance-plan: the inspection is a drainage check.
- Reopening log 2016-2025, https://example.org/ridge-line/reopening-log: one reopening date per year, without causes.
- Rider forum thread, https://example.org/ridge-line/rider-forum-thread: riders say the route reopens when the snow melts.

## Plan

1. One signature opens the route: name the engineer's sign-off as the act that reopens the Ridge Line.
2. The check behind it: show that the plan names a drainage inspection as the check.
3. Ten springs on record: show the logged dates and say they carry no causes.
4. Does snow decide?: set the rider belief against the documented check, without crowning either.
5. What we cannot tell yet: say that 2026 is undocumented and invite riders to share the dates they know.

The order goes from the person who acts to the proof, then to the doubt, so the open question is the last thing the reader keeps.

## Reservations

- The log stops at 2025.
- The forum thread is a rider's account, not an official source.

## Choix faits pour toi

- The engineer's sign-off leads — it is the one story two sources support.
- One carousel of five cards — five steps fit the sources without padding.
- The snow belief comes fourth — it is only attested by one forum thread.
