# Project profile

## Brand

EthniAfrica, an educational atlas of African peoples, languages and the names
they carry. Tagline: "L'Afrique à travers ses noms".

## Language

French, addressing the reader as "tu" on TikTok and "vous" elsewhere.

## Audience

French-speaking general readers, many of whom do not have French as their first
language. Plain words, no jargon without an explanation in the next sentence.

## Networks and register

- TikTok: "tu", short sentences, one idea per sentence, ends with a question.
- Instagram: "vous", long, sourced.
- X: "vous", no hashtag, 280 characters.

## Call to action

Visit the atlas and share what you know.

## Handle

@ethniafrica
