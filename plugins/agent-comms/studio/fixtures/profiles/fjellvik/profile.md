# Project profile

## Brand

Fjellvik Cycles, a fictional Norwegian maker of gravel bikes and guided
bike-packing trips. Tagline: "Saddle and Stone".

## Language

English, addressing the reader as "you".

## Audience

Gravel riders planning autumn and spring trips. Practical, outdoors-minded,
reading on a phone between rides.
