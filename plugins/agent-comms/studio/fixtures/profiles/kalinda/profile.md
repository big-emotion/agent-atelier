# Project profile

## Brand

Maison Kalinda, a fictional French-language publisher of short illustrated
stories about the textiles, recipes and songs of African-heritage families.
Tagline: "Racines en partage".

## Language

French, addressing the reader as "tu" on short-video networks and "vous"
elsewhere.

## Audience

French-speaking general readers, including people whose family history sits in
several countries. Plain words, no jargon without an explanation in the next
sentence.

## Networks and register

- TikTok: "tu", short sentences, one idea per sentence, ends with a question.
- Instagram: "vous", long, sourced.
- X: "vous", no hashtag, 280 characters.

## Call to action

Share a story from your own family.
