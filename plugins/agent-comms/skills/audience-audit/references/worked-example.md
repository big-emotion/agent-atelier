# A worked example — the first measured run

The baseline run of this audit on **EthniAfrica** (Big Emotion's own open atlas
of African peoples and languages), 2026-09-07, 30 days, consented sessions.
Kept here because it shows what the report's findings look like when they are
worth acting on — not as a benchmark to compare another site against.

## What was measured

- **55 unique visitors**, 71 visits, 219 pageviews, 3.08 views/visit,
  54 % bounce, 4m34 average visit.
- **Sources:** LinkedIn 25 (45 %), Direct 17, Google 11, Ecosia 1, one AI
  assistant referral. Three video platforms **absent**, despite three shorts
  published two days earlier.
- **Devices:** mobile 50 (65 % bounce, 2m10), desktop 5 (18 % bounce, 12m12).
- **Top pages:** the home page (29), one game route (15, **92 % bounce**, 1m46,
  76 % scroll), then hub pages at 4–6, then a long tail of records at 1–3.
- **Published corpus:** roughly 1 700 records across five content types —
  against roughly 50 URLs visited at all.

## The three findings that shaped everything downstream

1. **The site's second-most-visited page is a textbook dead end.** 92 % bounce,
   1m46 on page, 76 % scroll. The visitor consumed the whole thing and left.
   That is not a quality problem — the page worked — it is a missing next step,
   and it is the single most actionable line in the report.
2. **The channel carrying nearly half the traffic was scheduled last.** LinkedIn
   was 45 % of acquisition while the publishing plan treated it as a late phase.
   Measurement contradicted the plan, and the plan moved.
3. **91 % of visitors are on mobile, where they bounce nearly four times as often
   as on desktop.** On a mobile-first project that is a contract violation, not a
   demographic curiosity.

## What the absent channels actually meant

Three video platforms showed zero attributed traffic two days after publishing
three shorts. The correct reading was **not** "the videos did not work" — it was
that without campaign parameters those referrers are filed under Direct. The
finding was recorded as *unmeasured*, and tagging was the fix.

This is the difference the audit exists to make: a zero that means "we did not
measure it" filed as a zero that means "it did not work" sends the next quarter
of editorial effort in the wrong direction.

## What the numbers could not carry

Fifty-five visitors is a sample where one person's afternoon moves a page several
ranks. Every rank in that report survives; no precision does. And the whole
series counts consented sessions only — a floor of unknown depth, never the
audience.
