# LinkedIn copy (`descriptions.md`)

Written with `attention-architect`. Measured on EthniAfrica: on LinkedIn the
loop is opened **in the first line of text, above the fold** — the video does
not carry the hook, the post does.

## Rules

- **First line = the hook**, under ~200 characters so it shows before « voir
  plus ». Usually the speaker's strongest sentence, quoted, from our captions.
- **Attribute every thesis**: « pour lui », « selon Nadella ». Nothing the
  speaker argues is stated in BIG EMOTION's voice.
- **Verified role** for the speaker, the same as in the credit line.
- **One reframe**, clearly ours (« Ce que nous retenons… »): the sentence that
  turns the excerpt into a question the reader's business owns.
- **Close on a question the reader can answer from their own work**, about the
  idea, never about the person. Replies get answered within 48 h.
- Tutoiement. No sales pitch: the agency shows its reading, not its offer.
- URL in clear, three hashtags, credit line last.
